## Attribution

The six documentation principles in `skill/docbound/SKILL.md` are a distillation
of published engineering documentation guidance used across the industry and of
academic technical-communication practice.

The sentence-level patterns in `skill/docbound/references/anti-patterns.md`,
numbers 19 to 24, are adapted for technical documentation from the no-ai-slop
skill by Peter Yang (https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop), MIT licensed,
Copyright (c) 2026 Peter Yang. No text is reproduced from it; the patterns are
regrouped and rewritten for documents rather than for editing a human draft, and
the rules there about preserving a writer's voice do not apply to a document an
agent writes from a diff.

No text is reproduced from any of those sources, and the skill names none of
them: it is vendor-agnostic by design, and a documentation discipline that
arrives citing one organisation's house style invites the reader to argue with
the citation rather than with the rule.
